A VERY HUMAN BEGINNING

It began with what came home.

As a mother, Atika Yakubu saw used papers and exercise books coming home with her children. Term after term, the pages accumulated.

She imagined an organised way to recover that paper—and explore how its value could find a way back to education.

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Are you collecting paper now?

We’re developing our model and seeking partners. There is no confirmed collection service or pickup schedule yet.

How could schools benefit?

We’re exploring how recovered value could support educational resources. Any benefit will depend on the economics and an agreement with the school.

Can businesses order packaging?

We welcome enquiries while we explore manufacturing partnerships. Specifications, supply and availability need to be agreed before any order can be confirmed.
